# Executive Summary

## Cybersecurity Risk Governance Platform

### 1. Overview

The Cybersecurity Risk Governance Platform is an educational business analytics project that demonstrates how cybersecurity risks and security incidents can be organised, analysed and communicated through structured data and business intelligence reporting.

The project combines MySQL database design, SQL analysis, cybersecurity risk and incident registers, and a Power BI dashboard.

Its main purpose is to demonstrate how cybersecurity information can be translated into meaningful management-level insights.

### 2. Business Problem

Organisations need visibility into cybersecurity risks, incident severity, remediation status and potential business impact.

Without structured documentation and reporting, it can be difficult for management to understand which risks require attention and how incidents are distributed across departments.

This project demonstrates a sample reporting approach to address these information and visibility challenges.

### 3. Key Project Components

**Cybersecurity Risk Register**

The project includes five illustrative cybersecurity risks:

- Phishing Attack
- Ransomware Threat
- Weak Password Usage
- Insider Threat
- Malware Infection

Each record includes a department, likelihood, impact score, calculated risk score and mitigation plan.

**Incident Register**

The incident register contains sample security incident records with information such as incident type, severity, department, assigned analyst, status and date.

**SQL Analysis**

SQL scripts demonstrate database creation, data management, risk prioritisation and incident reporting queries.

**Power BI Dashboard**

The dashboard presents five KPI cards:

- Total Incidents
- Open Incidents
- Critical Incidents
- Critical Risks
- Total Risks

It also includes three visualisations:

1. Incident Severity Distribution
2. Department-wise Cybersecurity Risk Exposure
3. Cumulative Cybersecurity Incidents Over Time

### 4. Risk Assessment Approach

The project uses a simple risk scoring method:

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

Likelihood and impact are assessed on a scale of 1 to 5. This approach helps compare the illustrative risks and identify those with higher calculated scores.

### 5. Key Business Insights

The project demonstrates how structured reporting can help stakeholders:

- Identify and compare cybersecurity risks.
- Understand how risk exposure varies across departments.
- Review incidents by severity and status.
- Monitor cumulative incident records over time.
- Present cybersecurity information in a format suitable for management review.

The dashboard is intended to support visibility and discussion. It does not independently establish the effectiveness of security controls or predict future incidents.

### 6. Business Value

The project highlights the role of business analytics in cybersecurity governance.

By combining structured registers, SQL queries and Power BI reporting, it demonstrates how technical security information can be organised into business-oriented reports to support risk awareness, prioritisation and management discussions.

### 7. Tools and Technologies

- MySQL
- SQL
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel / CSV

### 8. Scope and Limitations

This is an educational portfolio project using illustrative sample data. Some incident details are simulated and are not verified organisational records.

The dashboard is not connected to a live security monitoring system. It does not provide real-time alerts, automated threat detection or production-level security monitoring.

The results should therefore be interpreted within the scope of the sample dataset and project objectives.

### 9. Conclusion

The Cybersecurity Risk Governance Platform demonstrates a practical application of business analytics to cybersecurity risk reporting.

It brings together risk documentation, incident tracking, SQL-based analysis and Power BI visualisation to illustrate how cybersecurity information can be communicated more clearly to business stakeholders and management.
